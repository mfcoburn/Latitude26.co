A small number of elective services sit outside the membership. They are
optional by design. Nothing here is necessary to your care, and nothing about
the membership assumes you will want any of it.

Where a member does, each is arranged individually and priced separately, and
discussed the same way the retainer is: in conversation rather than from a menu.

Each is provided under the same clinical standard as the rest of your care.
Where a treatment is elective, that is said plainly. Where the evidence behind
it is limited, that is said plainly too.
