Latitude 26 has no waiting room, because there is no office for you to visit.

Visits happen where you are. For most members that means home. For some it
means an office between meetings, or a boat at the dock. The clinical bag
carries what a well-equipped exam room carries. What it does not carry is the
hour of your day spent driving, parking, waiting, and completing a form you
have completed before.

Discretion is built into the model rather than offered as an accommodation.
Arrivals are unmarked and unremarkable. Nothing about a visit announces itself
to a neighbor, a doorman, or a colleague.
