Blood is drawn at your kitchen table rather than in a queue at a draw station.

What happens in the next two hours matters as much. Several common panels,
among them metabolic, lipid, and potassium, begin to drift the moment a sample
sits. Latitude 26 processes samples on site rather than carrying whole blood
across the county first, so what the laboratory measures is closer to what was
actually in your bloodstream.

Point-of-care testing answers some questions during the visit itself. Where
imaging is needed, it is arranged and pursued on your behalf. You are not the
one telephoning a radiology department.

Results come back as a conversation. Not a portal notification with a red flag
and no explanation attached.
