sections:
  - heading: The annual retainer
    body: |
      One retainer covers your care. There are no visit fees, no
      per-house-call charges, no after-hours surcharge, and no invoice arriving
      after the fact for a conversation you have already had.

      What it buys is not a quantity of appointments. It is a standing
      relationship with a provider who has the capacity to know you: available
      when you need them, unhurried when you meet, and coming to you rather than
      the reverse.

      Membership is held individually. Each person on the panel has their own
      place, their own record, and their own relationship with their provider. A
      spouse is not an addendum to your chart, and a child is a patient in their
      own right.

      Households are priced together, and at a meaningful reduction. A couple
      pays materially less than two individual memberships. A household with
      children pays substantially less again for each person on the panel: the
      per-person cost falls as the household grows, even though every person
      still holds a full place.

      The retainer may be paid annually or in monthly installments by automatic
      transfer, whichever suits you. Figures are discussed in your private
      consultation, where they belong.
  - heading: The exclusivity cap
    body: |
      Every panel at Latitude 26 is limited to 100 patients. However the
      practice grows, that number does not move. Adding a provider adds a panel,
      and never enlarges yours.

      The cap is a clinical decision rather than a marketing one. It is the
      number at which a provider can genuinely hold your history in mind: your
      baseline, your medications, your family's pattern, and what a change
      actually means for you rather than for a population. Above it, the
      arithmetic stops working and the care becomes something else.

      Because each person occupies a place, a household of four takes four of
      the hundred. That is deliberate. It is also why the panel fills faster
      than the number suggests.

      When a panel is full, it is full. Inquiries are held privately and in
      order, and are contacted as places open. Where an additional provider
      joins the practice and a new panel opens, those holding a place on the
      list are offered membership first.

sections_after:
  - heading: Term and cancellation
    body: |
      Membership runs for a year and renews annually.

      It can be ended at any point with thirty days' written notice. Any months
      paid for and not used are prorated and returned. There is no exit fee, no
      penalty, and no obligation to explain the decision.

      This is deliberate. A practice that has to hold members through a contract
      is not delivering what it promised. Latitude 26 would rather earn the
      renewal.

      Should you leave, your records are yours. A complete copy is provided at
      no charge, in a form your next provider can use.
