sections:
  - heading: Care where you are
    body: |
      The practice is named for the line of latitude that runs through
      Hollywood, where it is based. The coverage area extends roughly a
      half-degree north and south of it: Palm Beach at one end, Coral Gables at
      the other, with the practice sitting at the center of its own map.

      Within that area, care comes to you. Your home, most often. Your office,
      when the day does not permit otherwise. Your boat, at the dock or at
      anchor. There is no waiting room to sit in and no drive to make.

      For members who keep a second residence inside the area, a place in the
      city and a place on the water, care follows you between them. There is
      nothing to transfer and no one to notify.
  - heading: Beyond the service area
    body: |
      Membership does not lapse when you leave.

      Travel is when people most need a provider who knows them and least expect
      to reach one. Your provider remains available by phone, text, and video
      wherever you are: for advice, for a prescription, for a read on whether
      something can wait until you are home or needs attention where you are.

      Where local care is required, the practice helps you find it: identifying
      a credible provider or facility, briefing them on your history, and
      following up on what they find. What you should not have to do is explain
      twenty years of your medical history to a stranger in an unfamiliar city
      while unwell.

      For members who spend part of the year elsewhere, the membership continues
      uninterrupted through the months away, and resumes in person when you
      return. Visits at a second residence outside the coverage area can be
      arranged in advance where distance and schedule allow.
